## About

I develop software and reproducible research workflows at the intersection of **biblical studies, digital humanities, and engineering**. I work with Biblical Hebrew and Ancient Greek corpora, linguistic annotations, Text-Fabric, Jupyter, MCP servers, and interactive data visualizations.

This page is a guided entry point to my projects. **Expand a category** to see projects, or follow the **Try it** links to interact with a live application.

**Research principles**

- **Traceability:** connect results to original sources, datasets, and procedures.
- **Reproducibility:** document versions, environments, and analytical limitations.
- **Interoperability:** connect scholarly corpora to contemporary research interfaces.
- **Open access:** publish reusable tools and findings with appropriate attribution.
